# THE FRONTIER -- Discipline Abilities Draft

## System Overview

- **Attunement** at a Place of Power grants Ability 1
- **Discipline XP** earned passively by using the discipline in the field
- **Enhancement** at a City: spend accumulated XP to unlock next ability in sequence
- **6 abilities per discipline**, unlocked 1 through 6
- **7 disciplines, 42 total abilities**
- **3 picks from 7 = 35 possible builds**
- **P** = Passive (always active), **A** = Active (player-triggered)

---

## Survivalist
*Identity: Self-sufficiency in the wild. You stay out longer, move unseen, live off the land, and hunt with a bow. The wilderness is your home, not your obstacle.*

| # | Name | Type | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Trapper | A | Place snare traps that capture small game over time. Check traps after sleeping to collect food. XP earned per trap harvest. |
| 2 | Arrow Recovery | P | After combat, recover a percentage of fired arrows from corpses and the ground. Without Survivalist, missed and spent arrows are lost. The deeper the expedition, the more this matters -- you never run dry. |
| 3 | Night Eyes | A | Toggleable dark vision. Enhanced visibility in low light and darkness. Moderate stamina drain while active. |
| 4 | Light Foot | P | Reduced detection range from monsters. You can pass closer to territorial and ambush creatures before triggering aggro. Bow shots from stealth deal bonus damage. |
| 5 | Steady Draw | P | Bow draw speed increased. Aim sway reduced. Held draw drains less stamina. The Survivalist can draw, aim, and hold a shot where others rush and miss. |
| 6 | Weatherskin | P | Temperature survival thresholds widened significantly. Cold and heat affect you less. Reduces clothing dependency. |
| 7 | Endurance | P | Increased maximum stamina pool (+25%). Stamina regeneration penalty from fatigue is halved. |
| 8 | Ghost Walk | A | Short-duration full stealth. Monsters cannot detect you for ~10 seconds. Long cooldown. Breaks on attack or interaction. Open with a stealth bow shot for massive damage, then vanish. |

*XP earned by: trapping, bow kills, sleeping outdoors, traveling off-road, avoiding monster aggro at close range, recovering arrows.*

*The Survivalist's ranged identity: bows are silent (don't attract other monsters), effectively renewable (arrow recovery), and reward patience (stealth bonus, steady aim). Firearms are loud, expensive, and require settlement supply chains or Artificer crafting. The Survivalist doesn't need any of that. A Survivalist/Swordsman is a stealth archer who switches to precision melee when spotted. A Survivalist/Alchemist lives in the wilderness indefinitely, never running out of arrows or potions.*

## Ritualist
*Identity: Precursor channeler. Everything is a circle or a channel that manipulates space and objects. Ward excludes. Decoy attracts. Banishment Circle removes. Sending Chest teleports loot. Anchor teleports you. The Ritualist reshapes encounters through spatial manipulation.*

| # | Name | Type | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ward | A | 5-second channel. Places a 10-foot radius ward circle at target location. Monsters cannot enter the circle. Lasts 60 seconds. 15-second cooldown. Tactical tool: defensive shooting position, blocking retreats, holding enemies in AoE, safe corridors, camp defense. XP earned per ward placed. |
| 2 | Preserve | P | All decay rates reduced. Food spoils slower, weapon and armor durability degrades slower, potion potency lasts longer in inventory. Precursor energy slows entropy. |
| 3 | Decoy | A | 5-second channel. Place a ritual circle on the ground. After 10 seconds, an illusionary decoy appears and generates noise, attracting nearby monsters toward the circle. Use to pull monsters out of your path, bait them into a Banishment Circle, lure them away from camp, or create a distraction. Decoy persists for 30 seconds before fading. |
| 4 | Enchant | A | 5-second channel. Infuse your currently equipped weapon with precursor energy for 60 seconds. Increased damage and a visible glow. Stacks with runes. Works on any weapon type (melee, bow, firearm). |
| 5 | Sending Chest | A | 5-second channel. Conjure a magical chest on the ground. Place items inside. When you close the chest, it and its contents teleport to your home storage. One use per rest. |
| 6 | Banishment Circle | A | 5-second channel. Place a ritual circle trap on the ground. The first non-Apex monster to step inside is instantly despawned. Persists until triggered or next rest. Combine with Decoy: place Banishment Circle, then Decoy nearby to lure a monster into it. |
| 7 | Anchor | A | Two-part ability. (1) 5-second channel to set an anchor point at your current location. The anchor persists indefinitely until used. (2) From anywhere in the world, 5-second channel to teleport back to the anchor. The anchor is consumed on arrival. One-way, one-use. |

*XP earned by: completing ritual channels, visiting precursor sites, sleeping near precursor ruins, triggering Banishment Circles, luring monsters with Decoy, using Sending Chest, using Enchant in combat.*

*The Ritualist's progression arc: early game you ward camps and your gear decays slower. Mid game, Decoy lets you manipulate monster positions without fighting and Enchant gives you a combat edge. Late game, Sending Chest solves loot transport, Decoy + Banishment Circle creates automated kill zones, and Anchor teleports you home. Every ability is spatial: you place circles that do things, channel power into objects, and move things (including yourself) through space.*

## Pathfinder
*Identity: You read the land better than anyone. Travel lighter, recover faster, find what others need hirelings to locate. The exploration discipline. Where Survivalist is about wilderness survival and Ritualist is about precursor power, the Pathfinder is about covering ground and reading terrain.*

| # | Name | Type | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Wayfinder | P | Surveying tools reveal hexes at increased range (~+50%). You read terrain faster and further than anyone. XP earned per hex revealed. |
| 2 | Light Provisions | P | Food and water items weigh 50% less. You pack efficiently and waste nothing. |
| 3 | Cartography | A | At camp, produce a map of all hexes you have entered since last rest. Replicates Cartographer hireling function at no ongoing cost. |
| 4 | Prospect | A | Activates a sense that directs you toward the closest undiscovered resource in the current Area. A visual/audio cue points you in the right direction. You feel where the land is richest. |
| 5 | Steady Pace | P | Stamina regeneration rate increased (+30%). You just keep going. Where Warrior has burst stamina (larger pool), Pathfinder has endurance (faster recovery). |
| 6 | Pathsense | P | Precursor ruins and Places of Power within a moderate radius appear as a faint shimmer on the horizon, visible from high ground. You feel the old roads. |

*XP earned by: revealing new hexes, discovering resources, entering undiscovered Areas, producing maps, using surveying tools.*

*The Pathfinder's progression arc: early you survey further and pack lighter. Mid game you're producing your own maps and finding resources without a Surveyor. Late game, Pathsense makes you a precursor site detector from any hilltop. The Pathfinder + Survivalist is the ultimate deep-range explorer: self-sufficient on food and arrows, stamina that never quits, and a map that fills itself. Pathfinder + Ritualist with Anchor is "map everything, teleport home."*

## Warrior
*Identity: Direct combat power and physical resilience. You carry more, hit harder, and take punishment that would cripple others.*

| # | Name | Type | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Heavy Hand | P | Increased base melee damage (+20%). XP earned per melee kill. |
| 2 | Pack Mule | P | Increased carry weight limit (+30%). Over-encumbrance penalties are reduced. |
| 3 | Ironclad | P | Access to heavy armor tiers. Heavy armor is equippable (locked without Warrior). Damage reduction from all armor increased. |
| 4 | Stagger | A | Charged heavy attack that staggers any non-Apex monster, interrupting their current action and creating an attack window. High stamina cost. |
| 5 | Second Wind | A | When health drops below 25%, activate to instantly restore 30% stamina and gain temporary damage resistance for 8 seconds. Once per rest. |
| 6 | Warcry | A | Shout that causes all non-Apex monsters within 15m to flinch and briefly hesitate, creating a window to attack or disengage. Moderate cooldown. |

*XP earned by: melee kills, taking damage and surviving, carrying heavy loads, wearing heavy armor.*

## Swordsman
*Identity: Precision and finesse. You read the fight, exploit openings, and cripple monsters through technique rather than force. Master of the blade on both sides -- you know how to make cuts that bleed and how to avoid them. Gains bonuses with shields and off-hand weapons.*

| # | Name | Type | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Riposte | A | Successful dodge during a monster's attack window opens a counter-attack dealing 2x damage. XP earned per successful riposte. |
| 2 | Cripple | P | Riposte hits apply a cripple debuff: target's movement speed or a specific attack is disabled for 15 seconds. Debuff type depends on where the riposte lands. |
| 3 | Read | P | Monster attack telegraphs become more visible (enhanced visual tells, slight slow-motion on wind-up). Dodge window is slightly widened. |
| 4 | Off-Hand Mastery | P | Bonuses when using shield (increased block reduction, faster block recovery) or off-hand pistol (reduced swap time, aim bonus after melee combo). Makes off-hand items a core part of the Swordsman's kit. |
| 5 | Bleed | P | Critical hits (ripostes and backstabs) apply a bleed effect, dealing damage over 10 seconds. Stacks up to 3 times. Swordsman bleeds are more severe and last longer than baseline. |
| 6 | Blade Sense | P | Reduced probability of suffering Bleeding Gash and Deep Wound injuries. You understand edge weapons and know how to roll with a cut rather than take the full impact. |
| 7 | Flurry | A | After a successful riposte, activate to chain 3 rapid strikes before the monster recovers. High damage burst. Drains significant stamina. |
| 8 | Deathmark | P | Monsters you have crippled 3+ times in a single fight take 30% increased damage from all sources for the remainder of the encounter. Rewards sustained precision. |

*XP earned by: successful ripostes, dodges, killing monsters without taking damage, cripple applications.*

## Artificer
*Identity: Mobile blacksmith, field engineer, and master gunman. You carry raw materials and turn them into what you need on the spot. Your camp is a workshop. Your firearms are extensions of your hands.*

| # | Name | Type | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Field Repair | A | Repair weapons and armor at camp using raw materials (iron, leather, wood depending on item). Without Artificer, repair requires a settlement smithy. XP earned per repair. |
| 2 | Quick Load | P | Reload time for all firearms reduced by 30%. The multi-step reload animation plays faster. For most characters, reloading mid-combat is suicidal. For you, it's routine. |
| 3 | Ammunition Smith | A | Craft bullets and powder charges at camp from iron and sulfur. Turns raw mined materials into ammunition without needing a Town gunsmith. |
| 4 | Spike Trap | A | Craft and place spike traps from iron and wood. Significant damage to the first monster that steps on them. Place at chokepoints, camp approaches, or patrol paths. Recoverable if not triggered. |
| 5 | Steady Hands | P | Aim sway from fatigue reduced by 50%. Firearm accuracy at range improved. Combined with Quick Load, firearms become a primary combat weapon rather than an emergency option. |
| 6 | Reinforce | A | Strengthen a weapon or armor piece at camp, temporarily boosting damage or armor value until next sleep. Consumes materials. Stacks with runes. |
| 7 | Jury Rig | A | Improvise a temporary fix for any broken tool, weapon, or armor in the field without materials. Functions for ~10 uses. Emergency ability when out of repair materials. |
| 8 | Mechanical Insight | P | Precursor mechanisms (doors, locks, devices) that are inoperable for others can be activated by you. Opens shortcuts, hidden chambers, and locked rune caches. Also allows disassembly of precursor artifacts into rare crafting materials. |

*XP earned by: repairing equipment, crafting ammunition, firearm kills, placing/triggering traps, interacting with precursor mechanisms.*

*The Artificer's progression arc: early game you keep gear repaired and reload faster than anyone. Mid game you're self-sufficient on ammo, laying traps, and shooting with lethal accuracy even when exhausted. Late game, Reinforce over-buffs your gear for the hardest fights and Mechanical Insight opens parts of precursor ruins locked to other builds. The Artificer trades carry weight (raw materials are heavy) for field independence and firearm dominance.*

## Alchemist
*Identity: Botanical mastery and potion craft. You see what others miss, brew what others cannot, and your pack is a pharmacy.*

| # | Name | Type | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Keen Eye | P | Hidden gathering nodes (invisible to non-Alchemists) become visible. These yield rare ingredients unavailable otherwise. XP earned per rare ingredient gathered. |
| 2 | Light Load | P | All plant materials and potions weigh 50% less in your inventory. |
| 3 | Extended Potency | P | Duration of all potion buffs increased by 50%. A stamina tonic that lasts 3 minutes now lasts 4.5. |
| 4 | Toxicologist | P | Poisons and venoms are 50% less effective against you. Poisoning injuries are downgraded one treatment tier (town-tier becomes village-tier). |
| 5 | Master Brewer | A | Access to advanced recipes unavailable to others: mega-healing potions, long-duration temperature immunity, powerful monster repellent. Requires rare ingredients from Keen Eye nodes. |
| 6 | Identify | A | Examine any unknown plant, monster material, or precursor substance to learn its alchemical properties. Unlocks new experimental recipes. Without this, experimentation is blind. |

*XP earned by: gathering ingredients, brewing potions, using potions, experimenting with recipes.*

## Scholar
*Identity: You study the precursors where others merely use their power. Ruins become shelter, sustenance, and a library. The deeper you go, the more the continent gives back. Where the Ritualist channels precursor energy, you understand it.*

| # | Name | Type | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Runic Literacy | P | Precursor inscriptions (found throughout ruins) become readable. They reveal hints about the site's layout, hazards, and what's inside. Others see gibberish. XP earned per inscription read. |
| 2 | Salvage | P | Increased yield from precursor sites. You recognize valuable materials others overlook. Ruin loot tables improved. |
| 3 | Shelter | P | Sleeping in a precursor ruin counts as settlement-tier rest (Village equivalent). Injuries treatable at Village tier, full fatigue recovery, weather protection. Ruins become your camps. |
| 4 | Glyph Ward | A | Activate dormant precursor glyphs found in ruins. Creates a large safe zone (bigger than Ritualist Ward, longer duration, but fixed to the glyph location). Only works where glyphs exist (precursor sites). |
| 5 | Deep Reading | A | Study a precursor artifact to reveal the locations of related precursor sites on your map. You connect the dots between ruins, building a network of known sites. |
| 6 | Reactivate | A | Restore a dormant precursor sustenance system found in larger ruins. While active, fully restores hunger and thirst when used. Persists across visits but has limited charges before the system depletes permanently. A reactivated ruin is a supply depot. |

*XP earned by: reading inscriptions, studying artifacts, sleeping in ruins, reactivating systems, looting precursor materials.*

*The Scholar's progression arc: early you can read inscriptions and get better loot from ruins. Mid game, ruins become viable rest stops (Shelter) and you can lock down sections with Glyph Ward. Late game, Deep Reading maps the entire precursor network and Reactivate turns major ruins into supply depots. A Scholar who has mapped and activated several ruins across the continent has a personal infrastructure network that doesn't require settlements, roads, or hirelings.*

*Scholar + Ritualist is the precursor master: one understands, the other channels. Scholar + Pathfinder covers the continent fast and lives off ruins entirely. Scholar + Survivalist means you never need civilization at all -- food from traps, water from ruins, shelter from ruins, arrows from recovery.*

## Wizard
*Identity: Direct combat magic fueled by specific consumed reagents. Each spell requires its own materials. Power comes from preparation and supply management. Devastating when well-stocked, helpless when empty.*

| # | Name | Type | Cast | Reagent | Description |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Firebolt | A | 1s | Fire herb | Hurl a bolt of fire dealing moderate damage with a small burn DOT. Bread-and-butter combat spell. XP earned per spell cast. |
| 2 | Frost Snap | A | 1s | Crystal shard | Flash-freeze a target's legs, rooting them for 5 seconds. No damage. Creates distance or attack windows. |
| 3 | Force Push | A | Instant |Ite powder | Kinetic blast in a cone, staggering and pushing back monsters in range. Emergency crowd control. |
| 4 | Lightning Arc | A | 1.5s | Charged filament (monster part) | Chain lightning jumping between up to 3 targets. High damage. Excellent against swarms. |
| 5 | Stone Shield | A | 1s |Ite powder x2 | Conjure a barrier absorbing a fixed amount of damage before shattering (~15s or until depleted). Costs 2 reagents. |
| 6 | Ruin | A | 2s | Precursor core fragment | Massive single-target damage. The answer to Apex creatures. Consumes the rarest reagent for the biggest payoff. |

*XP earned by: casting spells, killing monsters with spells.*

**Reagent Specifics:**

| Reagent | Found In | Spells | Weight | Availability |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fire herb | Gathered plant (common in forests) | Firebolt | Light | Common. Buyable at Town+ |
| Crystal shard | Mined from deposits, precursor ruins | Frost Snap | Moderate | Moderate. Scattered in mountains and ruins. |
| Ite powder | Mined mineral (common in piedmont) | Force Push, Stone Shield (x2) | Light | Common. Buyable at Village+ |
| Charged filament | Monster drop (from electrical/ambush creatures) | Lightning Arc | Moderate | Uncommon. Must be hunted. |
| Precursor core fragment | Deep precursor ruins, rare caches | Ruin | Heavy | Rare. Available at Cities but very expensive. Also found through deep exploration. |

*Note: Reagent names are placeholder. The key design is that each spell has a specific, named material. The Wizard's pack before an expedition is a deliberate loadout decision: how many firebolts vs. frost snaps vs. lightning arcs can I carry, given weight limits and what I expect to face? Common reagents (fire herb, Ite powder) are cheap and light but fuel weaker spells. Rare reagents (core fragments) are heavy, scarce, and irreplaceable but fuel the most powerful magic available.*

*Shops sell common reagents (fire herb, Ite powder) at Town+. Uncommon reagents (crystal shards, charged filaments) occasionally appear at City shops but are unreliable. Rare reagents (core fragments) are available at Cities but very expensive -- finding them in the field is far more economical.*

## Cross-Discipline Notes

**Deliberate Overlaps:**
- Both Survivalist (Weatherskin) and Alchemist (temperature potions) help with temperature, but differently: passive resistance vs. active potion use
- Both Ritualist (Preserve) and Alchemist (Extended Potency) extend the useful life of consumables, but differently: Preserve slows all decay passively, Extended Potency increases active potion durations
- Both Warrior (Pack Mule) and Porter hireling increase carry capacity, but Pack Mule is permanent and free
- Both Survivalist (Trapper) and Hunter hireling provide food, but traps require player placement and checking
- Both Wizard and Alchemist want the same rare materials -- one burns them for combat, one brews them into potions. Choosing both creates internal resource tension.
- Both Survivalist (Arrow Recovery) and Artificer (Ammunition Smith) sustain their ranged ammo, but differently. Survivalist recovers spent arrows passively. Artificer crafts bullets from raw materials. Bows are silent and renewable. Guns are loud and powerful.
- Pathfinder (Cartography) replicates Cartographer hireling. Pathfinder (Prospect) partially replicates Surveyor for resource discovery (but NOT for founding). Saves money at the cost of an attunement slot.
- Both Pathfinder (Light Provisions) and Alchemist (Light Load) reduce carry weight for consumables, but for different item types: food/water vs. plants/potions. Taking both means your entire consumable pack is half weight.

**No Overlap (by design):**
- Only Swordsman gets riposte/cripple mechanics
- Only Artificer can repair gear and craft ammo in the field
- Only Alchemist can see hidden nodes
- Only Ritualist can place wards anywhere and set anchors
- Only Warrior can wear heavy armor
- Only Wizard deals direct magic damage
- Only Survivalist gets stealth/dark vision
- Only Pathfinder extends surveying range and senses precursor sites at distance
- Only Scholar can read inscriptions, sleep in ruins as rest, and reactivate sustenance systems

**Synergies Between Disciplines:**
- Swordsman + Warrior: Riposte into Stagger into Flurry = devastating combo
- Alchemist + Ritualist: Keen Eye finds hidden ingredients, Preserve keeps them fresh longer, Enchant + potions = double-buffed combat
- Survivalist + Artificer: Traps (snare for food) + Spike Traps (for damage) = camp is a killbox. Bow for stealth, gun for when things go loud. Self-sufficient on both ammo types.
- Artificer + Warrior: Reinforce on heavy armor + heavy weapons = the most durable, hardest-hitting melee build. Spike traps thin the herd before you wade in.
- Artificer + Wizard: Artificer carries raw materials anyway. Mechanical Insight opens rune caches. Spike traps buy time to cast. Self-sufficient on ammo for when spells run dry.
- Survivalist + Alchemist: Live off the land indefinitely (traps for food, Keen Eye for ingredients, Light Load for carrying it all, Arrow Recovery means arrows are effectively infinite)
- Survivalist + Swordsman: Stealth bow opener for massive damage, switch to riposte melee when spotted. The hunter-duelist.
- Ritualist + Any: Anchor enables the deepest expeditions. Set anchor at a City, push west until supplies run out or injury forces retreat, portal home. The Ritualist is the ultimate deep-range enabler regardless of second and third discipline.
- Pathfinder + Survivalist: The ultimate deep-range explorer. Self-sufficient on food/arrows, stamina that never quits, map fills itself, provisions weigh nothing. You can stay out indefinitely.
- Pathfinder + Ritualist: Map everything from extended range, sense precursor sites on the horizon, then Anchor home when done. The survey-and-teleport loop.
- Pathfinder + Alchemist: Light Provisions + Light Load = your entire consumable loadout weighs almost nothing. Maximum inventory space for loot and trade goods.
- Wizard + Warrior: Stone Shield + heavy armor = extreme tankiness; Stagger sets up Ruin
- Wizard + Alchemist: Keen Eye finds rare reagents others miss; but potions and spells compete for the same materials
- Wizard + Survivalist: Ghost Walk to reposition, then unleash spells from safety
- Wizard + Swordsman: Frost Snap roots target, Riposte when it breaks free. Force Push creates distance to cast.
- Scholar + Ritualist: The precursor master. Scholar reads and understands; Ritualist channels and uses. Glyph Ward secures ruins while Ritualist Wards cover the field. Deep Reading finds sites, Anchor lets you teleport between them.
- Scholar + Pathfinder: Map the continent, live off the ruins. Pathsense + Deep Reading = find every precursor site. Shelter + Reactivate = never need a settlement. Steady Pace keeps you moving between ruins.
- Scholar + Survivalist: Total civilization independence. Food from traps, arrows from recovery, shelter from ruins, sustenance from reactivated systems. You could play the entire game without visiting a settlement after your first Surveyor hire.
- Scholar + Wizard: Better ruin loot means more reagent finds. Shelter in ruins between spell-heavy expeditions. Deep Reading maps sites that contain reagent caches.

**Tension Between Disciplines (by design):**
- Wizard + Alchemist: Both want rare materials. Powerful combo for finding reagents, but every potion brewed is a spell not cast and vice versa. Forces hard resource allocation decisions.
